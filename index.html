⚠️ Why Android Says "Already Installed"

If you previously tapped "Add to Home screen", Android keeps that shortcut inside your phone's App Drawer (All Apps list) even if you removed it from your main screen. Swipe up to open your phone's App Drawer, search for PulseHabit, press and hold it, and tap Uninstall / Remove before installing again.
